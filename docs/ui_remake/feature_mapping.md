## Inventory totals and proposed additions

Final totals and newly proposed saved-but-unexposed controls belong in `PROGRESS.md` and `controls.csv`, not this mapping. The row annotations above count existing controls only; proposed additions remain identified and tracked in the inventory artifacts. The source-call inventory counts syntactic call sites, not widgets. Navigation/guide controls are inventoried in the misc controls CSV; this crosswalk does not duplicate them as extra shell-control rows. Other non-control surfaces (HUD, popups, dialogs and notices) are detailed in `other_surfaces.md`; Vesta page navigation remains mapped here as old page to proposed destination.
